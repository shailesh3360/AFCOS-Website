Our Services

Shore maintenance and engineering support for ships

01

Main Engine Overhaul & Repair

Top-end and general overhauls, running repairs, and troubleshooting for main propulsion engines. Our engineers carry out cylinder head, piston, liner, and bearing work to manufacturer and class standards, minimizing off-hire time.

02

Auxiliary Engine Maintenance

Servicing and repair of diesel generator sets to ensure stable onboard power supply, including fuel system checks, turbocharger servicing, and governor calibration.

03

Critical Machinery Maintenance

Maintenance and repair of pumps, compressors, purifiers, boilers, steering gear, and other essential shipboard machinery to keep vessels fully operational.

04

Shipbuilding & New-Build Support

Technical assistance and machinery installation support for shipyards during new construction, including commissioning and sea-trial support.

05

Shore Maintenance Planning

Planned maintenance scheduling for dry-dock and port-stay periods, coordinated with vessel operations to reduce downtime.

06

Emergency Breakdown Support

24/7 response for critical machinery breakdowns, with rapid deployment of engineers and spare parts to minimize vessel delay.
