About Abacus Marine Services

Trusted shore-side marine engineering partner

Who We Are

Abacus Marine Services Pvt Ltd is a shore maintenance company serving the shipping industry. We specialize in main engine and auxiliary engine repair, critical machinery maintenance, and technical support for shipbuilding projects. Our team of marine engineers works closely with vessel owners and shipyards to keep ships operating safely and efficiently.

From routine servicing to complex overhauls, our engineers bring hands-on sea-going experience ashore, ensuring every job is carried out to class and manufacturer standards.
